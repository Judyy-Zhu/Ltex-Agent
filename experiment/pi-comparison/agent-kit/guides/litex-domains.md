# Modeling obligations for later books (Litex)

These responsibilities match Lean; this guide supplies no target proofs. The approved initial interfaces are the minimal real plane and native algebraic foundation. No additional import, trust or foundation release is authorized here. Build missing definitions within the current budget or report incomplete work.

I-IV and VI: connect squared distance, distance, segments, rays, angles, circles, area, similarity and construction properties through checked definitions/proofs. Replacing equal lengths with equal squares requires nonnegativity and the correspondence in both directions. Expand orientation, collinearity, same-side and nondegeneracy constraints explicitly.

V: preserve kinds of magnitudes and original ratio-comparison conditions. A real quotient alone is insufficient without domains and an interpretation of ratio. Use explicit carriers/predicates to distinguish kinds and prove bridges to arithmetic representations.

VII-IX: distinguish the unit from a source "number". Natural/integer carriers must still preserve positivity, at least two units and other source restrictions. Prove correspondence for divisibility, multiples and ratio. Native accessible arithmetic facts remain subject to the approved foundation; do not substitute an equivalent target for the library-building task.

X: define commensurability relative to the prescribed reference magnitude. Distinguish length/square commensurability and all source irrationality classes. One generic nonrational-number predicate does not replace the classification.

XI-XIII: GeoPoint is two-dimensional, not a 3D point type. Build technical definitions such as cart(R,R,R), three coordinate projections and 3D vectors with explicit one-based indexing. Then build lines, planes, solids, areas, volumes, angles and exhaustion representations. All interfaces/helper proofs count toward cost. Other coordinate models are allowed if they preserve source objects, quantifiers and construction properties.
